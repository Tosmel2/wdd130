Our Rafting Adventures

Half-Day Splash

Perfect for families and beginners! Enjoy 3 hours of moderate Class II and III rapids. This scenic float provides plenty of excitement while allowing you to take in the breathtaking canyon views safely.

Full-Day Thrill

Ready for a challenge? Our full-day excursion tackles intense Class III and IV rapids. Includes a riverside BBQ lunch, cliff jumping opportunities, and 6 hours of adrenaline-pumping white water action.

Multi-Day Expedition

The ultimate wilderness experience. Spend 3 days navigating the entire river system with guided camping at night. We provide all the gear, meals, and campfire entertainment under the stars.
